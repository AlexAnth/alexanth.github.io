Child Safety Standards Policy

Effective Date: 01/09/2025
Last Updated: 01/09/2025

1. Commitment to Child Safety

Diafaneia app is committed to providing a safe, positive, and age-appropriate experience for all users, especially children. We strictly prohibit any content or behavior that exploits, endangers, or harms minors.

2. Age Restrictions

  • Our app is designed for users adults aged 18+.
  • Users under the minimum age required by law in their country must use the app with parental or guardian supervision.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from any of our users.

3. Content Standards

  • No sexually explicit, violent, or harmful material is allowed.
  • Content is reviewed to ensure it is age-appropriate.

4. Data Privacy and Protection

  • We comply with COPPA, GDPR-K, and other relevant child privacy laws.
  • We do not allow personalized advertising or tracking for children.

5. Advertising & Purchases

No ads or in-app purchases are offered via our application.

6. Reporting and Enforcement

  • We have zero tolerance for child exploitation.
  • Any suspected Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or harmful behavior will be reported to the appropriate authorities and Google immediately.
  • Users can report unsafe behavior or content directly through anthiswebdev@gmail.com.

7. Parental Controls and Guidance

Parents are always encouraged to supervise their child's use of the app.
