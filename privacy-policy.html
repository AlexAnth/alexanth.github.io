1. Introduction

Διαφάνεια στο Κοινοβούλιο (published by Alex Anthis) is an open-source, free Android application provided at no cost and intended for use as is. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles user data, in compliance with Google Play User Data policies.

2. Information Collection and Use

  • The app does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data.
  • No account creation is required.
  • No analytics, advertisement targeting, or data mining occurs.
  • No personal information is shared with third parties.
  • Documents retrieved or saved through the app are stored only on the user’s device.

3. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

  • No user data is retained on external servers.
  • All documents remain under the full control of the user on their own device.
  • If the app is uninstalled, locally stored files remain on the device and can be manually deleted by the user.

4. Security

Since no user data is collected or transmitted, there are no risks of unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration of user information.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically for any changes.
